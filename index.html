AdPilot India

Campaign creation for Indian SMBs, without the ad-ops overhead.

Generates complete Google and Meta campaigns from a plain-language business description in Hindi, Hinglish, or English. The product accounts for local festivals and city-tier context, not just the ad copy.

Problem

Indian SMBs need a lower-friction way to turn business context into campaign-ready assets across languages and regional markets.

AI & workflow

How I build AI products.

AI is only useful when it holds up inside a real workflow, with the right data, safeguards, and user experience around it.

01Understand the user & workflowWhat needs to happen?
02Shape the product and data modelWhat must be true?
03Design the AI interactionWhere is AI useful?
04Build for reliability & accessWhat can fail safely?
05Ship, observe, and iterateDoes it help?
Async queues for long AI work

AdPilot uses BullMQ + Redis queues so campaign generation can retry independently and the product stays responsive.

Data access belongs in the product design

MillOS uses 23-role RBAC and Postgres Row-Level Security because different operational roles must see different data.

Natural language needs a useful destination

BOS does not stop at a chat interface: its assistant queries live business data inside a CRM, task, and analytics environment.

AI output should enter a reliable pipeline

ViralCut turns an AI edit plan into a processing workflow through FastAPI, Celery, FFmpeg, PySceneDetect, and Remotion.
