About

About

I'm a full-stack AI engineer based in Bangalore, building production AI SaaS end-to-end. My flagship project, BOS SaaS, is live on Vercel — built solo from schema design to deployment.

Before AI engineering, I spent 2+ years in digital marketing and content. That background shapes how I build: I think about what the product actually does for the user before I think about what's technically interesting.

I've been an early adopter of AI tools since the GPT-3 era — hands-on testing across 200+ AI products including LLMs, voice agents, image generation models, chatbots, and automation tools. I've sent product feedback to teams when something didn't work right, long before AI engineering was a job category. That habit of breaking and stress-testing AI products gives me strong product intuition that I bring to everything I build.

I'm currently open to GenAI Engineer, AI Product Engineer, and full-stack (AI-focused) roles. Bangalore-based, open to remote.
